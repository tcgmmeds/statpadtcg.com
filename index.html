Features

Everything you need in one app

Collection & Binders

Add the cards you own, sort them into binders, and see what your whole collection is worth, with charts that follow its value over time.

Live Prices

Current market prices for every card in every set. Spot the most valuable cards and compare set totals at a glance.

Deck Builder

Build and manage deck lists in a visual editor. Start from scratch or import a list, with each deck's market value worked out for you.

Tournaments & Matches

Log every event match by match. Track your wins, losses and win rates, along with the leader you played and where you played.

Wishlist

Keep a list of the cards you're still hunting for, and choose whether your friends or everyone can see it.

Friends & Social

Add friends, follow their activity in a live feed, and share posts and stories with other players.

Accent Colors

Make the app yours. Pick one of seven accent colors and the whole app follows.
